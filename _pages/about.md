**Dr. Yang Ming (明洋) **currently is a Postdoc follow at The HongKong Polytechnic University, where he obtained his PhD degree. On Nov 2027, he will continue his research at IMDEA, Spain under MSCA-PF scheme. 

**His research interest includes: **

(1) Fibrous nanomaterials for multifunctional applications
  Design and fabrication of nanofibers by wet/melt spinning
  Understand the prediction of synthetic process by advanced techniques and simulations
  Application of fibrous nanomaterials for application e.g., metabolic monitoring

(2) Nanomaterials for electro/photocatalysts application
  Design and synthesis of catalysts with abundant active sites and facet for electrochemical reactions e.g., water splitting, nanoenzyme ;
  Understanding the intermediate status by simulations and in-situ techniques (Raman, ATR-FTIR)

  **His research skill includes:**
  
(1)   Synthesis skills: Experience in hydro/solvothermal synthesis; pyrolysis/carbonization; electrodeposition; electro/wet spinning; electroless plating and 3D printing.
(2)   Characterization skills: Skillful in use and analysis of various characterizations (XRD, BET, XPS, UPS, Raman,  FTIR,  TGA,  ICP-OES,  Zeta  potential/DLS,   SEM,  TEM,  PL,  UV-vis   spectroscopy,  Gas Chromatography,  AFM),  Skillful  in  in-situ  measurements  including  in-situ  Raman,  In-situ  surface enhanced ATR-FTIR, and electrochemical characterizations.
(3)   Modelling skills: Experience in Origin, Matlab, Prism; material structural modelling with Materials Studio, Shapr3D, DFT simulation by VASP and CASTEP, Programing language: Python.
